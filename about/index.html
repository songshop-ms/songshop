SONGSHOP

A two-location makeup studio that builds its own software with Claude

SONGSHOP is a semi-permanent makeup studio in Korea specializing in natural brows and scalp micropigmentation, with locations in Seoul (Jamsil) and Gangneung.

We are bootstrapped and run lean: instead of hiring an agency, we build the tools behind our marketing, content and operations ourselves, using Claude Code and the Claude API.

What we build with Claude

Since December 2025 we have created 31 repositories under github.com/songshop-ms, built with Claude Code.

  • Video ad engine — a Python pipeline that renders short-form before/after ads and procedure reels from raw studio footage.
  • Chat-driven video editor — a timeline editor with keyframes, versioning and color matching, where edits are made by talking to an embedded Claude Code agent.
  • Publishing studio — drafts posts for our Instagram accounts and translates them into Japanese, Chinese and English through the Claude API.
  • Design system — 53 reusable post templates for our feeds, published at songshop-ms.github.io/design_case.
  • Content research database — transcripts of 1,200+ short-form videos from our category, analyzed with Claude to plan scripts.
  • Landing pages — this website and our scalp landing page, with conversion tracking for our ads.
