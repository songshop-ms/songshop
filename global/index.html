Before you book

Questions

Will it hurt?

Almost not. We use numbing cream, so most guests feel very little.

How is it different from microblading?

Microblading cuts the skin to make strokes. Nano brows use a puncture method, so there’s less damage to your skin.

How long does it last?

About 3 years.

How long does it take?

Hair-stroke brows take up to 3 hours.

Do you work with international guests?

Yes — clients in Toronto, LA, Indonesia and Japan, including many mixed-heritage clients.
