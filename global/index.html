Nano brows vs microblading

Microblading cuts the skin. Nano brows don’t.

Microblading (the “emboss” style) draws strokes by cutting into the skin. Nano brows use a puncture method, so there’s much less damage to your skin — and the result lasts longer.

Will it hurt?

Almost not. We use numbing cream, so most guests feel very little.

What’s the difference from microblading?

Microblading cuts the skin to make strokes. Nano brows use a puncture method, so there’s less damage to your skin.

How long does it last?

About 3 years.
